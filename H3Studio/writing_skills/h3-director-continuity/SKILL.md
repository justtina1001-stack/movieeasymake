# H3 Director Continuity — Studio adaptation

## Writing rules
Honor prescribed events, shot order, duration and a requested continuous take. Each view observes new information: stimulus, subject change, camera response, a result and a handoff. Avoid changing framing merely to replay an action. Link adjacent views through movement direction, gaze, position, a held object, lighting, silhouette or a sound bridge. Keep identities, costume, handedness, object quantities, entrances and persistent states consistent. Express acting as stimulus, visible reaction, control or choice, and an after-effect with time to understand it. For physical action specify preparation, force or contact where needed, travel direction and the visible result; for a product or empty scene use structure, material and state change. Use reference assets only for their stated role, so incidental scenery or a reference pose does not become a universal requirement. Fit escalation and resolution within the original timeline. Do not impose dense-cut formulas, generate turnaround sheets, add software setup instructions, or replace the Studio's output schema with an upstream director table.
