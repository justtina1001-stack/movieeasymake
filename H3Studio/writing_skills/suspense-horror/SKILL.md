# Suspense / Horror — H3 Studio adaptation

## Writing rules
Establish the ordinary setting and available exit before revealing a concrete anomaly. Decide what the viewer sees before the protagonist notices it. Escalate through changed distance, blocked escape, a confirmed presence or an unexpected absence; do not repeat identical backward glances. Use over-shoulder, negative space or a motivated POV, preserving a pursuer's position only when the brief specifies a pursuer. Sounds, footprints and unexplained events do not automatically introduce a new character. Keep a single named protagonist as the same individual across angles; retain any separate shadow or figure explicitly requested in the story without duplicating the protagonist. Specify footstep rhythm, sound direction, a short silence and the reaction that follows. Reveal only what the brief needs. End with a specific escape, obstruction or unmistakable reveal, rather than a generic frightened face.
