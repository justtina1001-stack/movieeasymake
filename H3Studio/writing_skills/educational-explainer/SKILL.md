# Educational Explainer — H3 Studio adaptation

## Writing rules
Choose one question and one concept the viewer should understand. Use a concrete object, process or clearly identified visual analogy. Reveal the starting condition, a single causal change and its result in order; align narration with what is currently visible. Keep narration short enough to include pauses and comprehension time. Do not invent real-world data, technical capabilities or historical facts; state missing facts as unresolved in planning rather than fabricate them. Do not depend on perfect generated labels or dense text. End by showing the answer or the completed step.
