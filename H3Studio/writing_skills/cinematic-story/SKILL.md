# Cinematic Story — H3 Studio adaptation

## Writing rules
Choose one protagonist and one immediate desire. Show an ordinary opening state, a disruption, a choice or attempt, and its consequence. Introduce only the people, props and places needed for that change. Let a wider view establish space, then use a motivated closer view for the decision or reaction. Show the ending through an altered pose, object or relationship. Replace abstract emotion with gaze, hesitation, hand movement and distance; every beat must change the situation rather than repeat it.
