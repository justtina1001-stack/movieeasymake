# Emotional Drama — H3 Studio adaptation

## Writing rules
Define the relationship, the immediate emotional obstacle and one small decision. Use a supplied or compatible meaningful object to make the conflict visible. Show a glance, breath, interrupted reach, changed hand grip or altered distance instead of merely saying that someone is sad or moved. Give the actor time to react. Move closer only when intimacy or the decision requires it. Dialogue should add something the visible action cannot express. End with a changed relationship shown by who moves closer, leaves, accepts or releases the object.
