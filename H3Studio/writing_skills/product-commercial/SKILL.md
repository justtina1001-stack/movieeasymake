# Product Commercial — H3 Studio adaptation

## Writing rules
Identify the intended viewer's single need and one benefit supported by the supplied brief. Open with a visible situation or striking product detail, demonstrate the actual use, then show the resulting benefit. Preserve referenced shape, color, markings and proportions through handling and camera changes. Avoid unsupported performance, health, price or numerical claims. End with an unobstructed, settled product view. Treat exact logos, prices and call-to-action typography as post-production elements unless supplied for the scene; do not make generated text the only explanation of the benefit.
