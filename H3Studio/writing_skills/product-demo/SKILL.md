# Product Demonstration — H3 Studio adaptation

## Writing rules
Establish the initial product state, the operator and an uncluttered working area. Select at most a few steps that fit the duration. Specify the acting hand, contact point, control movement and the product's resulting state in order. Keep the essential mechanism visible and do not hide it with fingers, dramatic camera motion or unnecessary cuts. Preserve orientation, accessories and color. Demonstrate only provided functions. Hold the final result long enough to compare it with the opening state; use synchronized clicks or mechanical sounds where appropriate.
