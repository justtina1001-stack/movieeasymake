# Comedy / Reaction — H3 Studio adaptation

## Writing rules
Make the character's intention and the important prop clear in the setup. Create one physical misunderstanding, timing error or reversal that contradicts the expectation. Keep the cause and result visible in a stable composition; cut only to information needed for the joke. Reserve a brief pause after the payoff, then show an unmistakable reaction through eyes, hands, balance or posture. Add a final small consequence only if timing permits. Specify contact, cloth or object sounds without duplicating dialogue or relying on a laugh track.
