# Fantasy Adventure — H3 Studio adaptation

## Writing rules
Establish the explorer's route, goal and scale relative to the unknown place. Reveal the extraordinary element through approaching, uncovering or touching it, not an unrelated montage. Make one world rule visible through a physical response. Introduce one obstacle or risk and a decision that follows from the discovery. Keep magical light and particles subordinate to the character action, with a stated source and timing. Maintain the entrance, path and landmark positions across cuts. Finish on the consequence of the choice or a clearly motivated next step.
