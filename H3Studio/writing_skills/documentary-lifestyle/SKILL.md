# Documentary / Lifestyle — H3 Studio adaptation

## Writing rules
Choose one observable activity or environmental change and a clear point of view. Start with the place or context, show the activity, then a meaningful detail and its result. Let natural movement and location sound provide rhythm. Motivate camera position and movement as an observer; retain the selected visual style instead of silently switching it. Use only supplied real names, locations, histories and factual claims. Distinguish a fictional reconstruction when applicable; do not manufacture interview quotations or lived experience. End with the activity's result or a changed environmental detail.
